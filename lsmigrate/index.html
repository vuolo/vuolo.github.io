Why this

The official tool moves part of the store. The rest is engineering.

1

Links and webhooks do not redirect

Lemon Squeezy’s FAQ says all checkout links, storefront URLs, embedded checkouts, API endpoints and webhook events must be moved to Stripe or another provider, and that there are no automatic redirects.

2

Managed Payments leaves gaps

Their comparison and migration guide say Managed Payments does not include PayPal, storefronts, digital fulfillment (files, license keys, download links) or affiliates. Checkout overlays and custom checkouts need to be updated to Stripe Checkout or Payment Links.

3

Fixed price, written scope

One price for a defined scope, delivered in 5–10 business days. That is the technical work. The official 4–8 week window still applies for customer notice (at least 14 days before subscriptions move) and cleanup.
