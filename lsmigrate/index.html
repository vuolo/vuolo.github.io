Official timeline

The March 31, 2027 deadline will not be extended.

Lemon Squeezy says existing stores keep working until that date, and that an end-to-end migration, including notifying customers, updating links and integrations, and cleaning up remaining subscriptions, typically takes 4 to 8 weeks. Why this

The official tool moves part of the store. The rest is engineering.

1

Links and webhooks do not redirect

Lemon Squeezy’s FAQ says all checkout links, storefront URLs, embedded checkouts, API endpoints and webhook events must be moved to Stripe or another provider, and that there are no automatic redirects.

2

Managed Payments leaves gaps

Their comparison says Managed Payments does not include PayPal, storefronts, digital fulfillment (files, license keys, download links) or affiliates. It also says to confirm that your business location and products meet Managed Payments eligibility before you migrate. A store that does not meet it needs a different destination.

3

Fixed price, written scope

Entry is $399, Standard is $750, Full is $1,500, each for a defined scope. Standard and Full are 5 to 10 business days of build work. The official 4 to 8 week window still applies for customer notice (at least 14 days before subscriptions move) and cleanup.

How it works

A written intake, then a fixed-price build

1. Written intake

A checklist of what you sell, how customers pay (including PayPal), whether you use license keys, file delivery, affiliates, overlays or a custom checkout, and which destination is even eligible.

2. Written scope and price

Entry, Standard, or Full, and one of Stripe, Polar, Creem, or Paddle, in writing, before any paid work. If none of the four fits, I’ll say so.

3. Build and test

Products and checkout on the destination we agreed. Subscribers move only by the path that provider documents today. Where a customer has to check out again, the scope includes canceling the old subscription so they are not billed twice.

4. Handoff

A short walkthrough, a list of every old Lemon Squeezy link that was replaced, and notes for the customer notice Lemon Squeezy will send (at least 14 days before subscriptions move).
