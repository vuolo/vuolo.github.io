How it works

Five business days, four checkpoints

Day 0: written intake

You tell me the 4 to 12 jobs an agent should do in your product, and which are read-only.

Day 1 to 2: tools + auth

Typed tools over your API, scoped keys or OAuth, a working demo in Claude Desktop.

Day 3 to 4: hardening

Validation, rate limits, error messages an agent can recover from, contract tests in CI.

Day 5: handoff

PR to your repo, install docs, listing copy, a 30-minute walkthrough.
